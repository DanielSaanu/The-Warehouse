CONSIDER (defensible but worth changing; why; how):
- **Holder memory cap shape.** Flat "N most recent" forgets a quiet player the moment a busy one arrives -> cap per (holder, player) at 2-3 entries with an LRU over at most ~32 remembered players per holder: 43 × 32 × 3 × 60 B ≈ 242 KB, bounded and it still remembers you.
- **Two 80-line modules and a pre-emptive split.** `Wildlife` 80 and `Targeting` 150 exist to stay under a ceiling nothing has hit; with H2's 15-line header plus requires, ~20% of an 80-line file is boilerplate -> keep `Economy` (it buys ownership), fold `Wildlife` into `Bodies`, and split `Targeting` out of `Brains` when `Brains` actually passes 250.

- **Missing H9: new logic goes in `shared/` as pure Luau unless it touches a Roblox API; `server/` modules are thin adapters.** It is the only rule that keeps the AI able to check its own work without Studio, `CLAUDE.md` already requires it of `shared/`, and the plan's `Population`/`Economy`/`Standing` sit on top of pure cores (`Families`, `Trade`, `Reputation`, `Witness`) that are already there.

ANSWERS (to §8's still-open questions):
- **What must catch-up record?** A capped world-level headline ring, not a per-player diff. `meta.headlines[]` of `{ day, kind, subjectId }`, 64 entries ≈ 2.5 KB; the player key stores `lastSeenDay`; the "you were gone eleven days" line is built at join from headlines newer than that, filtered to tribes the player has standing with. `t.news` (one string per tribe) is this idea already, un-generalised. Per-player diffs are exactly the per-holder-per-player growth §5 warns about.
- **Is DataStore's serialiser denser than JSON?** No — the value is serialised to JSON and the 4 MB limit is measured after serialisation, so there is no free win; worse, a sparse numeric map becomes string keys (`"1234":5` ≈ 11 B/entry). Density has to come from your own packing: `WorldGen.encode`'s `packBytes` already gets tile data to ~1 B/tile, and the same trick on the diff gives ~4 B/entry. Budget in JSON bytes, as the plan does.
- **Real death rate per in-game day?** Bounded below the plan's assumption, so the arithmetic is safe. Only registered villagers enter the registry (`Sim.lua:689-691`; group members and animals never do), and refill is birth-only: `CONCEIVE_CHANCE 0.5` per couple per 7-day roll, ≤4 couples per village, 3 villages → ≤ ~0.9 births/in-game-day sustained, with `MAX_PEOPLE` holding the living at 27. A massacre is a burst, not a rate: the 21-day gestation-plus-childhood pipeline caps long-run growth at the birth rate. ~15,300 records ÷ 0.9 ≈ 17,000 in-game days ≈ 118 real days. People are indeed not the thing to watch.
